Engineering Overview & Applications

This 4-page VRM Structures catalogue presents the PM Surya Ghar BOS and mounting kit: one source for the modules, inverter, structure, distribution boxes, cables and earthing of a residential rooftop system. Kit Contents (3 kW / 5 kW): - Solar Modules: Adani, Waaree, ReNew Power or Vikram Solar (6 modules for 3 kW, 10 for 5 kW) - Inverter: Polycab, Growatt or Deye (1) - ACDB-DCDB with MCB: MCB & SPD, L-Max make (Polycab) (1) - Earthing Kit (Terrabond): 1 lightning arrester, 3 earth rods, 3 earth pit chambers, 3 chemical bags of 10 kg - Structure for RCC / high-rise roofs, VRM Structures make (1) - Polycab 4 sq mm DC cable, red & black (40) and Polycab 4 sq mm AC cable, red & black (40) - Orbit 16 sq mm green earthing cable (90 for 3 kW, 100 for 5 kW) - MC4 connectors (2), cable ties, lugs and glands (5), PVC cable tray Service Promise: End-to-end support, on-time delivery, complete BOS supply, nationwide service network, expert technical assistance and hassle-free procurement.
