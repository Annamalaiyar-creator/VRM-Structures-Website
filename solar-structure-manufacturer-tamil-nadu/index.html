Made in our Chennai factory

Our in-house design and fabrication team, roll-forming and fabrication lines are at Nagappa Industrial Estate on GNT Road, Puzhal. Structures are designed for each site, fabricated and galvanized in-house, and supplied as kits ready for installation. Our FRP walkways alone are supplied to more than 250 EPC solar contractors across India.

Built to standard

  • Hot-dip galvanizing to IS 4759:1996, maintained at 650 GSM (80–90 µm) as per the MNRE standard
  • 150 GSM Galvalume (YS 550 MPa) and 120 GSM pre-galvanized steel options
  • Aluminium 6063-T6 rails for metal, Klip-Lok, Kal-Zip and standing-seam roofs
  • Every structure designed with FEA and STAAD Pro analysis for a wind speed of 47 m/s
  • Penetrative and non-penetrative (adhesive) roof mounting

For installers, EPCs and dealers

Rooftop installers get structures for RCC, metal-sheet, tile and asbestos roofs, with clamps, earthing and walkway accessories from one supplier. For PM Surya Ghar projects we supply complete BOS kits for 3 kW and 5 kW systems. EPC companies get site-specific design with STAAD Pro reports for ground-mount, carport and custom structures.
